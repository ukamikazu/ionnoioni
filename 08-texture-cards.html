Texture Cards

Every texture in the S class can be softened with ta or intensified with ra, and where the particle stands says where the softening or intensity falls: at the start (how it arrives), at the end (how it lingers or builds) or on both sides (all the way through). Each card below gives the word, its Siddham, its base reading and its six bracketed forms. Select a word to open its card.

Read the six forms as a ladder. Gentle throughout is the mildest, the base word sits in the middle and intense throughout is the strongest. Where a familiar clinical term exists, it is used first.

These readings are not absolute, only a ballpark of the texture's grain, left to personal interpretation as in most language except technical vocabulary such as anatomy. ta and ra are never mixed in one form.
