The Everyday World

This page gathers the words for doing things, for the living world and the sky and for the qualities of things: how big, how long, how full, how true. Nearly all of them are built from words the language already had.

Doing things

The body at work

A body part with -tui is that part at work, so the postures fall out of the body words on their own.

New verb roots

A few actions needed roots of their own. They use the open slots in the verb shape, and their sounds follow the old colors: tr- for the lighter, quicker acts, kr- and pr- for the harder ones, au for what hurts and oi for what alarms.

The qualities of things

Scales

Five scales have a pair of short words each. The ending names the scale and the vowel names the end of it: e, the more open sound, for more, and i, the narrowest, for less. The difference is a single vowel, which makes them very good material for tongue twisters.

lengthern, long𑖊𑖨𑖿𑖡𑖿irn, short𑖂𑖨𑖿𑖡𑖿

truthers, true𑖊𑖨𑖿𑖭𑖿irs, false𑖂𑖨𑖿𑖭𑖿

prin stays the everyday word for little and goes on doing its work inside compounds such as prinseti (finger). int is the measured opposite of ent.

How many

Quantity is the plural particle u with the brackets on it, a ladder from a few to all.

PhraseSiddhamMeaning

Other is se, the sideways direction: psa i se is the other stone, and pan i se is another.
